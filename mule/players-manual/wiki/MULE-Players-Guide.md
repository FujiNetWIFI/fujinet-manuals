# M.U.L.E. Player's Guide

*The player's guide to The FujiNet Multiplayer M.U.L.E., in an edition for each machine it runs on.*

---

M.U.L.E. is played over the Internet with FujiNet. Up to four colonists share a room. Right now there is one room, **Planet IRATA**, and more will be added. Each guide below covers the same game, with its own machine's pictures, controls and loading steps.

- [[Atari 8-bit|MULE-Players-Guide-Atari-8-bit]] (Atari 400, 800, XL and XE computers)
- [[Apple II|MULE-Players-Guide-Apple-II]] (Apple //e (enhanced, 128K), //c and IIgs)
- [[Color Computer|MULE-Players-Guide-Color-Computer]] (TRS-80 Color Computer 1 and 2)
- [[MS-DOS|MULE-Players-Guide-MS-DOS]] (IBM PC (CGA) and IBM PCjr)
- [[Coleco Adam|MULE-Players-Guide-Coleco-Adam]] (Coleco Adam)
- [[MSX|MULE-Players-Guide-MSX]] (MSX1, MSX2 and later)
- [[Atari Lynx|MULE-Players-Guide-Atari-Lynx]] (Atari Lynx)
- [[Intellivision|MULE-Players-Guide-Intellivision]] (Mattel Intellivision)
- [[Bally Astrocade|MULE-Players-Guide-Bally-Astrocade]] (Bally Professional Arcade / Astrocade)
- [[Atari 2600|MULE-Players-Guide-Atari-2600]] (Atari 2600 VCS)

A printable PDF of each guide is available as well.
